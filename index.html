LOG 01 — IN DEVELOPMENT (UNREAL ENGINE 5)

Backrooms: Command

Run a retail store by day. Fund a research empire into the Backrooms by night. Direct hired expedition teams through camera feeds from your HQ — and decide when to pull them out before something notices them.

The Pitch

Every existing Backrooms game puts you inside the liminal space, alone, running from something. Backrooms: Command puts you behind the monitors instead — hiring, equipping, and managing teams who go in so you don't have to. It starts personal, though: before any of the management systems unlock, you go in yourself.

How It Plays

  • Five Levels, Five Identities Normal Backrooms, the Poolrooms, an Abandoned Office, the Fun Level, and the Infinite Field — each with its own danger profile and mechanics.
  • Live Camera Feeds Physical in-world monitors, not menu overlays. What you see is what your HQ actually sees.
  • Research Economy Six research pools feed a sprawling tech tree that gates new equipment, personnel, and access.
  • Personnel Under Pressure Hired crew run on a three-state behavior model — Observe, Flee, Resume — reacting to what they encounter on the feed.
  • Consequences That Compound Health risk, equipment cost, and government attention all scale with how deep you push.

LOG 02 — CONCEPT DOCUMENT

Relic Age

Long after civilization collapsed, survivors fight and build with scavenged fragments of technology they no longer understand — treated now as relics, rituals, and myth.

The Pitch

Relic Age is a real-time action-RPG set in a medieval world built on the ruins of a futuristic one. Superstition has replaced science. Somewhere above it all, ruling factions are quietly protecting the truth about how everything actually fell apart — and you're about to start finding pieces of it.

How It Plays

  • A Fractured World A handful of major factions and many smaller tribes, each with different access to old-world tech — mapped onto real-world geography reimagined as post-collapse.
  • Sword vs. Gun Real-time combat built around a counter dynamic between relic melee weapons and the guns restricted to major factions only. Your relic sword is one of a handful left in existence.
  • Two Tiers of Loot Common relic fragments grant incremental upgrades; major relics from boss fights unlock entire skill branches.
  • Parry or Dodge A branching skill tree in the spirit of God of War — competency across both styles, mastery in one.
